## 1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how Echo Pro collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Account Information
- Email address
- Password (hashed with bcrypt)
- Name (optional)

### 2.2 Chat Data
- Messages sent to and from the AI
- Session titles
- Timestamps

### 2.3 Technical Data
- IP address
- Device type
- Browser information
- Error logs (via Sentry)

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

- Provide chat services
- Improve AI responses
- Maintain security
- Send service updates (with consent)

## 4. DATA STORAGE

- All data stored on secure servers (Railway/Render)
- Database encrypted at rest
- Backups retained for 30 days

## 5. DATA SHARING

We do NOT sell your data. We may share data with:
- Moonshot AI (for processing chat messages)
- Hosting providers (for infrastructure)
- Legal authorities (when required by law)

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Export your chat history

## 7. SECURITY

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- JWT authentication
- HTTPS encryption
- Rate limiting
- Regular security audits

## 8. COOKIES

We use minimal cookies for:
- Session management
- Authentication tokens

## 9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Echo Pro is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy. Users will be notified of significant changes.
